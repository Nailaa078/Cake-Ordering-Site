About us

Minimal, modern desserts made by hand, one order at a time.

Small bakery, big on detail

COCO by Haani is an online cake and dessert shop making minimal, modern buttercream cakes, éclairs, tarts, cupcakes, macarons and all sorts of breakfast pastries.

We are based in London. Every product is handcrafted and made to order, so tell us the flavour, size and date and we will bake it for you.
